SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Share Purchase Agreement is made on 12 March 2024 between PT Nusantara Holding as seller and PT Meridian Capital as buyer.

The seller agreed to sell 100,000 shares for a total purchase price of IDR 25,000,000,000.

The completion date was 30 April 2024. On 15 May 2024 the buyer sent a notice alleging that the seller failed to deliver the share register.

The seller denies any failure and states that the register was delivered on 28 April 2024.

Exhibit 9: a further meeting occurred on 3 July 2024.
